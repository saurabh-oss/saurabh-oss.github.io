04 — Point of View
/ 01

Architecture is a service to business outcomes, not a monument to engineering taste.

/ 02

AI doesn't replace good design — it raises the floor and demands better systems thinking around it.

/ 03

Open source is the most reliable form of future-proofing. Bet on the community, not the vendor cycle.
